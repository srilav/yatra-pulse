        How to book

        Official tickets only · no agents
        1. Check your itinerary firstAdd the events to My itinerary. Trip check warns you about clashes at different venues and gaps too short to travel. "Book tickets" runs the same check before opening the official site.
        2. Open the official siteGo to mysoredasara.gov.in and tap Book Tickets. TicketGenie is the only authorised seller.
        3. Pick a ticket and paySign in, choose your seat and pay online. You get a booking confirmation by email or SMS.
        4. Collect the real ticketExchange the confirmation for your physical ticket or Gold Card at the Spandhana Counter, Deputy Commissioner's Office, Mysuru. Bring a government photo ID.
        5. On the dayCarry the ticket and the same ID. Jamboo Savari gates: 10 am–12:30 pm. Torchlight parade gates: 4:30–6:30 pm.
